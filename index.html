Team Builder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

In short: Team Builder does not collect, send or share any data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

To help you manage your team, Team Builder saves the information you enter, such as:

  • player names, shirt numbers and preferred positions
  • whether a player is available or borrowed from another team
  • formations, saved lineups, set-piece takers and Game Day details such as substitutions

This information is saved only in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We have no server and no account system, and we cannot see your data.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information about you or your players.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties.
  • The app doesn't need an internet connection and makes no network requests.

Exports, backups and imports

When you choose Export as JPG or Export Backup, the app opens your device's share sheet. The file only goes where you choose to send it, for example Files, Photos, AirDrop or email. Once you share it, that app or service's own privacy policy applies.

When you import a CSV roster or a backup file, the app reads it on your device and doesn't send it anywhere.

Children's information

Team Builder is made for coaches and team managers. Rosters may include the names of junior players. That information stays on the coach's device and is never collected by us. We suggest using first names or shortened names, which the app does automatically when importing a roster.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual players or lineups in the app at any time. Deleting the app from your device removes all of its data permanently. Because we never hold a copy, we can't recover it for you, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds syncing between devices, we'll update this page and the date above before that version is released.
